# Photo reconstruction notes

Reference: ten user-supplied classroom photographs, filenames below. This is a
manually interpreted, stylized reconstruction. It is not a photogrammetric scan
or a measured architectural survey.

## Interpreted layout

- Long cream-painted block room with a suspended tile ceiling, light panels and
  blue-lit skylight panels.
- Single tall window on the far short wall; a closed exit door to its left and a
  tall wooden cabinet to its right.
- Wooden-topped cabinet benches along the left wall; gray-topped black lab tables
  to the right of the aisle; red and yellow metal stools.
- Whiteboard, mobile ViewBoard, projector, clock, flag and colored-paper pockets
  on the right teaching wall.
- Side preparation-room doorway and robotics shelves toward the back.
- Lower-ceiling teacher nook, rear exit, desk, storage cabinet and four enclosed
  printers at the rear.
- Scalloped light strings and blue floor boundary tape.

## Estimates and simplifications

The main room is modelled as 7 × 14 m with a 3.6 m ceiling; the rear nook has a
2.65 m ceiling. These dimensions are estimates based on the apparent block,
ceiling and furniture proportions. Bench and stool positions were inferred
across overlapping viewpoints. Portable furniture may have moved between photos.

The side prep room is only a shallow suggested recess, because its full extent
is not shown. Exterior corridors are not modelled. Papers, screens, posters and
small accessories use reconstructed generic content. Furniture, machines and
printers are simplified geometry rather than manufacturer CAD.

The reference photos remain outside this repository. They are not texture maps.

The highest-value calibration measurements are room width/length, ceiling and
soffit heights, one wooden bench's width/depth, and the distance from the rear
wall to the prep doorway. A dimensioned sketch would settle placement ambiguity.
